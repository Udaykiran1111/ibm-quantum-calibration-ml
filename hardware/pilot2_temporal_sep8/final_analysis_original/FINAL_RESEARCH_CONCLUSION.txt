EXPERIMENTAL QUALIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The hardware comparison is empirical and backend-specific. It should not be interpreted as proof of universal superiority.

Transpilation depth is an important experimental confound. Some random-control circuits required deeper transpiled circuits than the Sweet-Spot circuit, which can independently reduce GHZ fidelity.

The final hardware analysis excludes the old Kingston job from the primary experiment and uses the selected primary dataset.

OVERALL CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The completed evidence supports the 30-day temporal model as the preferred Sweet-Spot selection model. The model provides a reproducible telemetry-based mechanism for ranking candidate physical qubits and selecting topology-valid 3-qubit chains for hardware evaluation.

The hardware experiment provides empirical evidence about whether the selected Sweet-Spot chain outperforms random controls on the evaluated IBM Quantum backends. The result must be interpreted together with transpilation depth and other hardware-level experimental factors.
